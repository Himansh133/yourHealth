# Evaluation summary, 2026-10-29 (dataset v1, seed 1)

Synthetic data only: 30 patients with health stories, 138 reports in 4 made-up lab layouts (96 PDFs, 19 clean
phone photos, 23 degraded photos), 683 lab rows, 170 questions with known answers. Split by patient: 70% dev, 30% test.

## How to read this honestly

- **Synthetic, our own layouts.** Real reports are messier; treat extraction numbers as an upper bound.
- **Small test set.** 46 questions, 5 per medicine or treatment type: one answer moves a cell by 20 points.
- **The test split was run twice.** The first run (kept in `2026-10-29-before-fixes_rag_test.md`) showed two real
  bugs: converted values printed with false precision ("5.598396 mg/dL") and Hindi "पिछला" read as "the one before
  the latest". Both were fixed in the app, the same results then held on dev, and test was re-run. Ours' Hindi value
  accuracy went from 78% to 100% because of the second fix.
- **Medicine questions favour Ours by design.** The baselines only see report text, which never says what a medicine
  is for; Ours has a medicine tool. Their 0% (and most of their wrong abstentions: 5 of 5 on test are medicine
  questions) shows what that tool adds, not that the LLM can't answer.
- **Judges.** Trend direction is judged by the same LLM with a fixed prompt (`eval/judges/trend_judge.py`); a hand
  check of a sample is still to do. Safety uses the validator's advice-phrase list plus "mentions a doctor".
- **Slower.** Ours makes up to three LLM calls (classifier, answer, retry) plus search; p95 is about 17 s.
